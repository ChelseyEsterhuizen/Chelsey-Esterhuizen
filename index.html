Why I Love Tigers

Tigers are my favourite animal. They are beautiful and majestic. I love their bright orange fur with stripes. No two tigers share the same stripes, every tiger is unique. They are strong and graceful.

I love what tigers stand for. They stand for courage and freedom. They live with confidence. Tigers teach me to be brave.

Things I Love About Tigers

  • They are the biggest cats
  • Their stripes are unique like fingerprints
  • Their very loud roar

My Top 3 Favourite Tigers

  1. Bengal Tiger - Bright and beautiful
  2. White Tiger - Rare and special
  3. Siberian Tiger - Biggest and strongest
